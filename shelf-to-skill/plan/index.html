Getting the most out of each book

Habits that make it stick

One book, one artifact

Every technical book ends with something public: a repo, a notebook, or a post. It forces you to finish and it becomes your portfolio.

Exercises over highlights

For the spine books, the exercises are the reading. If a chapter has none, rebuild its main example from memory.

Pair math with its use

Read the matching Lay or Rosen chapter only when the ML book needs it. The math books become support, not a second queue.

Flashcards for math and language

Ten minutes of spaced repetition a day keeps formulas and kanji from fading between blocks.

Teach it back

After each block, explain the main ideas in a 10-minute recording or a one-page summary. Gaps show up fast.

Rearrange the shelf by track

Give the current books their own "now" shelf at eye level. Move finished books to a "done" row so progress is visible.

Let certs set deadlines

Network+, AWS Cloud Practitioner and AWS ML are already on your shelf. An exam date is the best pace setter there is.

Clear the duplicates

CLRS 3rd edition, Lay 3rd edition and Python Machine Learning 3rd edition are superseded by newer copies you own.
